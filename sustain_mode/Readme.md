# Sustain mode

## Purpose of the Sustain Wellness mode

The Sustain Wellness mode supports people (primarily 45–75+) who want to age with capability, stability, and confidence. It focuses on sustaining daily independence and long‑term health by reinforcing balance, rhythm, cardiometabolic resilience, and recovery. It is distinct from Activate Wellness (life‑readiness today) and from short‑term Transform (time‑boxed intensity).

## Core Dimensions of Sustain

We anchor Sustain in four measurable, research‑aligned dimensions that Cirdia can assess with wrist-worn sensors and light, optional self‑tests:

- Stability (Balance & Gait) Everyday steadiness and safe movement: gait rhythm, stride variability, turning smoothness. Falls risk reduction lives here.
- Rhythm (Circadian & Sleep Regularity) Consistent sleep/wake timing, sleep continuity, and overall sleep sufficiency that supports hormonal and metabolic health.
- Vitality Resting heart rate trend, activity consistency (moderate minutes), and blood‑oxygen stability proxies.
- Resilience (Allostatic Load & Adaptation) HRV baseline and stability over weeks, day–night HR/HRV patterns, and how quickly variables normalize after stressors.

Why these four? Balance and rhythmic regularity strongly predict healthy aging trajectories; vital activity and robust recovery buffer or create resilience against decline.

## Sensor → Metric Mapping (Passive by Default)

| **Dimension** | **Passive Sensors (Free)** | **Premium Add-Ons (Optional)** |
|----------------|-----------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Stability** | Accelerometer/gyroscope: stride-to-stride variability, step symmetry; turning smoothness during walks; incidental sit-to-stand detection | Guided 4-meter gait-speed timing (phone timer); 10-sec one-leg stance (camera-free, phone-timed) |
| **Rhythm** | Sleep onset/offset regularity (std. dev. of timing), sleep fragmentation, nocturnal HR; wrist temp trend as circadian proxy | Weekly rhythm coach: bedtime window nudges; optional chronotype questionnaire |
| **Vitality** | Resting HR trend; weekly moderate minutes; intensity distribution; daytime SpO₂ stability (where available) | VO₂-proxy walk test using HR + cadence + distance (phone-timed) |
| **Resilience** | Morning HRV (e.g., rMSSD) baseline and coefficient of variation; HR recovery after free-living exertion | Guided 1-minute recovery snapshots after an optional brisk climb/walk |
